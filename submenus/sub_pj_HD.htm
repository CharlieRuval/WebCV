Hospital design
A hospital layout was planned and drafted in AutoCAD to support a safe, functional, and efficient healthcare environment, aligned with NOM 001, NOM 002, NOM 007, NOM 016, and NOM 087 requirements. The design integrated architectural and structural considerations together with regulatory constraints to ensure compliance and operational practicality.
Lighting distribution was analyzed in detail to ensure adequate illumination across clinical and administrative areas, reducing visual fatigue and minimizing shadows in critical workspaces such as operating rooms and laboratories.
In addition, biosafety measures were incorporated to prevent hazardous biological infectious waste from entering non clinical areas, strengthening containment, handling, and disposal practices. The layout also included the routing of medical gas pipelines, with attention to accessibility, redundancy, and emergency safety provisions, while maintaining alignment with applicable health and safety standards.
Electrical installations were planned to support both standard and emergency operations, including backup power integration and dedicated circuits for high demand equipment. Overall, the project emphasized technical accuracy, regulatory adherence, and practical workflow needs to support effective clinical operations and patient safety.
