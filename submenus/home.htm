About me | This website showcases my journey, projects, and ongoing pursuit of innovation through interdisciplinary learning

I am a Biomedical Engineer with a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering from CINVESTAV del Instituto Politécnico Nacional and a Bachelor's degree in Biomedical Engineering from Tecnológico de Monterrey. My academic training has provided a solid foundation in automation, control systems, and biomedical technologies. I am motivated by complex engineering challenges and maintain a strong commitment to continuous, independent learning across multiple disciplines. Through programming, advanced problem-solving, and the exploration of scientific topics, I consistently seek to expand my technical expertise and broaden my perspective.
